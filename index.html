AI Audit: Common Questions

What is an AI Audit?

A short, practical review of how your business actually runs and markets itself. We identify the specific tasks and marketing dollars where AI can save time or make money now, then rank them by payback speed. You get a written list of opportunities with estimated time and cost savings.

How much does the AI Audit cost?

The AI Audit is free. It takes about 15 to 30 minutes of your time, and you keep the findings whether or not you hire us.

Do I need technical knowledge to use AI in my business?

No. We teach AI in plain English and set up the tools for you. Most owners start with everyday tasks like writing marketing copy, answering common customer questions, and cleaning up their reporting.

How fast can a small business see a return from AI?

The opportunities we prioritize are the ones that pay back in weeks, not years, such as cutting wasted ad spend, reclaiming staff hours on repetitive work, and making your website easier to find. We measure the results with you.

What do you need from me for the audit?

Just a conversation. It helps if you can describe where your customers come from, roughly what you spend on marketing each month, and which tasks eat the most time. No system access is required.
